# Theme Sector Tickers 5-day RS

How the score is built

Each theme's score is a liquidity-weighted average of its constituents' 5-day return — tickers that trade more dollar volume per unit of volatility carry more weight.

  1. 01Universe. Themes and their tickers are parsed from themes.md; only those symbols are scanned out of the large/mid/small-cap daily parquet files, in lazy mode, to keep memory low.
  2. 02Dollar volume. close × volume per bar, then a 20-day rolling mean per ticker.
  3. 03ATR20. True range — max(high−low, |high−prev_close|, |low−prev_close|) — rolled over 20 days per ticker.
  4. 04Liquidity weight. 20-day dollar volume divided by ATR20, then normalized within each theme so its members' weights sum to 1.
  5. 055-day return. Percent change of adjusted close vs. adjusted close 5 trading days prior, per ticker.
  6. 06Theme score. Weighted sum of member returns — the number plotted above.
